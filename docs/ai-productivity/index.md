# AI Productivity

> *"The goal isn't to use AI more—it's to accomplish more with AI as your partner."*

---

## The 5 Levels of AI Productivity

Every team member—developers, QA engineers, designers, business analysts, project managers, and tech leads—can progress through these five levels. Each level builds on the previous, unlocking new capabilities and efficiency gains.

<ProgressionTracker />

---

## Why This Framework?

Most AI training focuses on **what tools exist**. This framework focuses on **how you work with AI**—a progression that applies regardless of which specific tools you use.

The levels aren't about AI sophistication. They're about **integration depth**:

| Level | Integration | Value |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Chat** | Separate from work | Ad-hoc assistance |
| **Context** | Manual bridging | Better answers |
| **Connected** | Embedded in workflow | Seamless assistance |
| **Customized** | Tailored to your needs | Multiplied efficiency |
| **Autonomous** | AI as teammate | Transformed output |

---

## Getting Started

### Assess Your Current Level

Most people operate at **Level 1 (Chat)** or **Level 2 (Context)**. That's okay—the goal is progression, not perfection.

Ask yourself:
- Do I copy-paste between AI and my work tools? → **Level 2**
- Does AI have direct access to my codebase/files? → **Level 3**
- Have I customized AI with project-specific prompts? → **Level 4**
- Can AI complete multi-step tasks with my review? → **Level 5**
