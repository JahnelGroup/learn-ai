# Models Family (G5)

**The noble gases of AI—stable foundational capabilities that everything else reacts around.**

The raw intelligence that powers the entire table. While other families describe how to use AI, this family describes what AI fundamentally is: the models themselves, their capabilities, and their variations.

---

## Family Characteristics

- **Foundational capability:** The core reasoning engines
- **Stable but evolving:** New models constantly push boundaries
- **Provider-dependent:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, open source

## Learning Path

**Foundation:** Understand [LLMs](/docs/periodic-table/models/llm)—how they work, their capabilities, their limitations.

**Practitioner:** Work with [Multi-modal](/docs/periodic-table/models/multi-modal) and [Small Models](/docs/periodic-table/models/small-models)—matching models to use cases.

**Expert:** Leverage [Thinking Models](/docs/periodic-table/models/thinking)—the frontier of AI reasoning.

---

## Key Insight

The Models family teaches you that **not all AI is created equal**. A $0.001/token model and a $0.10/token model solve different problems. A model that processes images opens different use cases than one that doesn't. Understanding the model landscape helps you make informed architecture decisions rather than defaulting to "use GPT-4 for everything."
