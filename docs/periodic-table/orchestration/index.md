# Orchestration Family (G3)

**These elements coordinate multiple components into working systems.**

You can't orchestrate one thing—this family only exists through combination. Orchestration is about the glue: how context flows, how components connect, how systems are built from primitives.

The Orchestration family turns individual AI capabilities into complete solutions.

---

## Family Characteristics

- **Combination-focused:** Always involves multiple components
- **System thinking:** The whole is more than the sum of parts
- **Integration patterns:** How pieces fit together

## Learning Path

**Foundation:** Understand [Context Windows](/docs/periodic-table/orchestration/context)—the fundamental constraint of LLMs.

**Practitioner:** Build with [RAG](/docs/periodic-table/orchestration/rag) and [Frameworks](/docs/periodic-table/orchestration/frameworks)—production AI patterns.

**Expert:** Explore [MCP](/docs/periodic-table/orchestration/mcp)—emerging standards for AI integration.

---

## Key Insight

The Orchestration family teaches you that **AI systems are architectures, not just models**. A model alone rarely solves a business problem. You need to orchestrate retrieval, manage context, handle errors, and connect to external systems. This family is where engineering meets AI.
